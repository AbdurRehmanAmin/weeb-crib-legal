Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Weeb Crib is a non-commercial app for tracking anime, donghua, manga, manhwa, manhua and light novels with friends. It is run by one independent developer. This page explains what the app stores about you, why, and how to get it back or delete it.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, or the account ID from Google or Discord if you sign in with them. We never see your Google or Discord password.
  • Profile: username, display name, bio, avatar, banner, colorway and your settings.
  • Library and activity: what you track, scores, progress, notes, custom lists, reviews, stamps, predictions, votes and tier lists.
  • Social: who you follow and block, posts, replies, likes, community memberships, drop-room messages and reactions.
  • Chats: direct messages, Crew messages and the photos you send in them. Chats are stored on our server and are not end-to-end encrypted.
  • Device: a push notification token when you allow notifications. Messages you write while offline stay on your phone until they are sent.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no tracking SDKs, no analytics, and we never sell or rent your data.
  • We don't read your chats. An admin can see a message only after someone reports it, to decide whether it breaks the rules.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts the database, file storage, sign-in and realtime chat.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver push notifications using your device token.
  • Google and Discord handle sign-in if you choose them.
  • GIPHY returns GIF search results. Searches go through our server, so GIPHY doesn't receive your account details.
  • AniList, MyAnimeList, AnimeSchedule and news sites provide catalog and schedule data. When you import from MyAnimeList or AniList, we fetch the public list for the username you enter.

Who can see what

  • Your profile, posts, reviews, room messages and public library entries are visible to other members.
  • With a private account, only followers you approve see your library and posts. Entries you mark private are visible only to you.
  • Chats are visible only to the people in them.

Your controls

  • Download your data: Settings → Account → Download my data gives you a JSON copy of your profile, library, posts, reviews, messages, lists, stamps and follows.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently erases your account, content and uploaded photos. This can't be undone.
  • You can turn off push notifications and activity sharing, go private, mute or block people at any time.

How long we keep data

  • We keep your data while your account exists. Deleting your account removes it straight away; database backups roll over within 7 days.

Age

  • Weeb Crib is for people aged 13 or older. Adult catalog entries are hidden.

Changes and contact

  • If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app will tell you before it takes effect.
  • Questions, data requests or problems: email abdurrehmanamin130@gmail.com.
